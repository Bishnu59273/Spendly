# Spendly - Organic Social Content Plan

> **Goal:** Grow from 13 organic social sessions/month to 100+ in 90 days.
> **Approach:** 3 platforms, 4 content pillars, one drafted post per slot.

---

## Platforms & Cadence

| Platform        | Frequency | Best Times (IST)        | Voice                       |
| --------------- | --------- | ----------------------- | --------------------------- |
| **Twitter / X** | 5×/week   | 8–9am · 1–2pm · 9–10pm  | Direct, witty, relatable    |
| **LinkedIn**    | 2×/week   | Tue–Thu 7–9am or 12–1pm | Founder perspective, honest |
| **Instagram**   | 3×/week   | 7–9pm                   | Visual, screenshot-first    |

---

## Content Pillars

Four themes - rotate so no two consecutive posts share a pillar.

| #   | Pillar                   | What it does                                                        |
| --- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 🪞  | **Relatable Moments**    | Makes people say "this is literally me." No product push.           |
| 🔦  | **Feature Spotlights**   | One feature, one real problem it solves.                            |
| 💡  | **Finance Tips**         | Standalone advice - no Spendly mention. Builds trust.               |
| 🏗  | **Builder Transparency** | Metrics, decisions, what you learned. Indians love founder stories. |

### Wednesday · LinkedIn · 💡 Product Insight

```
We asked 50 Spendly users why they stopped using their last expense tracker.

The top answers:
- "Too many steps to log an expense" (61%)
- "Forgot to track for 3 days and gave up" (48%)
- "Couldn't understand the reports" (39%)

None of them said "not enough features."

The enemy of a good finance habit isn't complexity. It's friction.

Everything we build at Spendly starts with: how do we reduce friction?
```
